# About The Ilantic Journal

> **Status: in formation.** The journal is not yet accepting submissions. This document describes the intended design. Details are subject to change, and several are deliberately left open (see [What is still open](#what-is-still-open)).

The Ilantic Journal is a proposed academic journal organized around the **theoretical research program** rather than the individual paper. It is meant for theoretical work whose value lies in what it makes possible for others: the questions it allows a field to pose, and the research that could not have been built without it.

The research vision emerged from Setaleur Foundation and was set out by Momen Ghazouani, Founder and CEO of Setaleur. The Ilantic Journal was founded on August 10, 2025.

## Why the paper is not always the right unit

The paper suits most of science: a bounded argument, a discrete claim, a contribution that can be read, evaluated, and filed. It suits less well the work that changes what a field is able to ask. Three problems follow.

- **The unit is wrong.** A bounded argument cannot carry a framework whose value lies in the questions it makes possible, so the architecture disappears into the results built on top of it.
- **Priority is hard to secure.** Long theoretical work pushes authors to publish early and incomplete in order to claim the idea as theirs.
- **No one keeps the account.** Journals rarely return to ask what a published idea went on to build, or whether its predictions held.

## Three commitments

We know of no journal that combines all three.

### 1. The theoretical research program

A program must do four things:

- **Name the wall.** State, as precisely as possible, what researchers in the domain could not do before, and why.
- **Build the path.** Provide a developed theoretical architecture, not only a direction: one a researcher could work within without relying on the author for interpretation.
- **State what follows.** Make explicit the research lines it opens and the questions it reframes, including at least one consequence that is testable in principle.
- **Account for what it displaces.** Identify the assumptions it renders obsolete and show that the field's prior practice actually rested on them.

### 2. Adversarial review, published in full

Instead of conventional peer review, independent panels are asked for one thing: the flaw that would bring the program down. They are not asked for suggestions or minor criticisms. If they cannot find such a flaw, they document where they looked and why each line of attack failed. That full record is published beside the program.

### 3. The Living Record

Formal reassessments at five and ten years account for what a program produced: the research lines it opened, where its predictions held or needed revision, and which fields it reached, including ones its author did not anticipate. These are accountings, not retrospective critiques.

## How a program moves through the journal

1. **Program Architecture Document.** Before any component is submitted, the author submits a complete structural map of the program: the wall it addresses, the components it requires and why each is necessary, the predictive structure expected on completion, and a timeline.
2. **Adversarial review of the architecture.** The document is reviewed before any component is considered.
3. **Priority date.** When the document is accepted, its acceptance date is recorded publicly as the program's date of theoretical priority. This is a public record of when a design survived review, not a legal instrument. Its purpose is to remove the pressure to publish incomplete work just to establish authorship.
4. **Components.** Each component is evaluated on its own terms and on whether it is necessary to the accepted design. Components follow binding deadlines (currently proposed: the second within three years of the first, later ones within two years of the previous). A program that misses its deadlines is publicly marked as suspended, and what was already published stays in the record.

## Two tracks, one standard

- **Track I: the established program.** Programs first disseminated at least five years before submission, where the subsequent record makes their fertility assessable. These submissions must show actual yield, not only architecture.
- **Track II: the advancing program.** Original programs that meet the same structural criteria but whose consequences cannot yet be assessed. They are clearly marked as such, and what is assessed is the architecture.

## Editorial principles

Every editorial decision is meant to answer one question: **does this program change what the field is able to ask?**

- **Generativity first.** A program is judged by what it opens, not only by what it settles.
- **Durability over novelty.** The aim is to accept work that would still be necessary decades later, not work that is merely timely.
- **The architecture precedes the reception.** Evaluation looks at the structure of the program, not at the author's prior recognition, institutional affiliation, or existing citations.

The journal expects to decline more submissions than it accepts, and in some periods it may accept none. That follows from holding a high standard, and is not a flaw in the process.

## Who it is for

The journal is for researchers whose work operates before the usual cycle of hypothesis and confirmation: where the questions themselves are being formed and the vocabulary of an inquiry is being established. It is also for readers who want to understand not only what a field concluded, but what made those conclusions reachable.

## What is still open

We prefer to show where the design is firm and where we want partners' judgment:

- **Funding model and fees.** Undecided. Any model should avoid tying revenue to acceptance decisions.
- **Initial scope.** Which one or two fields to begin with.
- **Review panels.** Composition, recruitment, and compensation.
- **Independent work.** How to give a fair hearing to programs that arrive without institutional backing, for example through additional review time, without compromising the principle that the program, not its author, is what is evaluated.
- **AI tools.** Their role in review and reassessment.
- **Governance.** How editorial independence is protected.

## A likely sequence

An indicative order only. No dates have been set.

1. **Pilot.** Apply the method to established programs with a five-year record, where the outcome can already be checked.
2. **Narrow opening.** Open to new programs in one or two fields, with a small founding panel.
3. **Wider scope.** Extend field by field as the published record supports it.
