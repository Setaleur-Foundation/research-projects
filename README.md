# The Ilantic Journal

> **Status: in formation.** The journal is not yet accepting submissions. All details described here are subject to change.

The Ilantic Journal is a proposed academic journal that publishes **theoretical research programs** rather than individual papers. It is built for the kind of theoretical work that changes what a field is able to ask, and that the paper format carries poorly.

## The idea

The journal rests on three commitments:

1. **The theoretical research program as the unit of publication.** A program names the barrier a field could not pass, builds the path beyond it, and states what follows and what it displaces. Authors first submit a *Program Architecture Document*; its acceptance fixes a public date of theoretical priority.
2. **Adversarial review, published in full.** Independent panels are asked for one thing: the flaw that would bring the program down. Every attack attempted, and why it failed, is published alongside the program.
3. **The Living Record.** Formal reassessments at five and ten years account for the research lines a program opened, where its predictions held, and which fields it reached.

## What is settled and what is open

**Settled:** the program, not the paper, is the unit of publication; review is adversarial and its record is public; there are two tracks under one standard (established programs with a five-year record, and advancing programs assessed on architecture).

**Open:** the funding model, the initial scope (which one or two fields to begin with), the composition and compensation of review panels, the role of AI tools, and the governance that protects editorial independence.
