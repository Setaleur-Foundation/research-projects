# Setaleur Foundation Research

This repository brings together research projects and preliminary findings developed by the **Setaleur Foundation**, offering an early look at the ideas, frameworks, and inquiries shaping its work.

## About the Foundation

Setaleur Foundation is an organization affiliated with Setaleur, established to advance long-term initiatives that serve the public interest through research, knowledge, innovation, and institutional development.

The Foundation was previously known as Setaleur Unity, a nonprofit division within the Setaleur ecosystem, and has since evolved into a full institutional foundation with a broader organizational mandate. While operating within the wider Setaleur ecosystem, it maintains independent administration and organizational management.

The Foundation supports the development of ideas, principles, and frameworks that address societal, technological, educational, and intellectual challenges. It may conduct research, establish or support initiatives, and oversee institutions whose activities align with its mission and principles. It seeks to preserve the long-term integrity, independence, and continuity of the initiatives it supports, while allowing them to operate through appropriate organizational and economic models.

Its work is guided by a commitment to public benefit, intellectual integrity, responsible innovation, and sustainable institutional development.
